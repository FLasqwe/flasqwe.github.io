03

Four tides

Every engagement runs the same four phases. Fixed scope per phase, a decision at every turn, no surprises on the invoice.

  1. 01

    Ebb

    Research · 2–3 weeks

    The water pulls back and shows the bottom. Interviews, data, competitors, the uncomfortable truths. We leave with a brief everyone has signed.

  2. 02

    Turn

    Strategy · 2 weeks

    Positioning, message, creative territory and the roadmap. One deck, one decision meeting, one direction.

  3. 03

    Flood

    Build · 4–10 weeks

    Identity, site, product, campaign. Weekly demos of real work, not mood boards. You see it move before it ships.

  4. 04

    High water

    Launch & grow · ongoing

    Launch, measure, iterate. Growth retainers start here and pay for themselves or they stop.
