Our story

One fire. Every dish.

OGNIVO began with a single question: what if a kitchen gave up every modern shortcut? Our hearth is lit at 4 pm and never goes out during service. Each dish is built around its own heat — embers for fish, open flame for meat, the cooling oven for bread and dessert.

We work with twelve small farms within 200 km. The menu changes with what they bring, sometimes twice a week.

The chef

Mark Vetrov

“Fire doesn't forgive shortcuts. You can't rush embers or fake smoke — and guests feel that honesty on the plate.”

Eleven years in kitchens from Copenhagen to San Sebastián. Mark learned live-fire cooking in the Basque Country and opened OGNIVO to bring that discipline home.
